# LuxCore native NVIDIA juice-scene photographic-exposure comparison

This is an upstream LuxCore 2.11.2 stock 3-D juice/glass scene, not a TSS oil projection or fluid simulation. `juice-exposed.png` is an actual 480×270 8-bit RGB render. It shows two heavily noisy colored objects against a mostly white background. It is not a photorealistic liquid-light result.

This guarded single job repeated the all-white [baseline](../windows-gpu-full-scene-20261119/EVIDENCE.md). The only captured configuration differences were the fresh output filename and image-pipeline plugin 0: `TONEMAP_LINEAR` changed to `TONEMAP_LUXLINEAR` with upstream photographic settings exposure `0.0028571428265422583`, f-stop `4`, and sensitivity `100`. Plugin 1 remained `GAMMA_CORRECTION` at `2.2`; vignette, chromatic aberration and OIDN were not enabled. The pinned LuxCore console source loads `.cfg` properties then applies the `-D` command-line properties; the v2.11 manual documents these photographic parameters. The logged configuration diff confirms no other differences.

The baseline decoded to 388,800 white RGB bytes; this image contains 19,809 non-white bytes. This controlled comparison confirms a visual change under the specified tone mapping, but does not alone establish that the baseline's entire failure was caused by its exposure settings or validate physical light transport.

Render settings: `PATHOCL`, `opencl.devices.select=0100000`, `film.hw.enable=0`, `opencl.native.threads.count=2`, 480×270, `batch.haltspp=32`, `scenes/juice/test.cfg` plus `scene.file=scene.scn`. The log identifies one NVIDIA GTX 1650 Ti `(Type = OPENCL_GPU)` intersection device and two Native CPU threads. The OpenCL kernel was cached; LuxCore reported `Done` at 4.000 s. It used the same reservation, UUID / sampled-idle preflight and 900-second best-effort deadline as the prior run; the guard released after successful process exit. No macOS GPU job was run.

Four NVIDIA telemetry samples, 1081–2241 ms apart, recorded a sampled maximum of 100% utilization, 313 MiB reported memory, 50°C and 25.81 W. These observations may miss peaks and do not guarantee device/driver recovery. The reservation coordinates only participating jobs, not other graphics applications.

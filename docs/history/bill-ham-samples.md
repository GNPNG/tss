# Bill Ham's visual references

The artist's still gallery pairs screenshots credited to emi with photographs credited to Larry Lauterborn. Inspect the credited original for each comparison. A photograph includes the recording lens and screen, and an online still alone cannot establish the optical cause of a fringe. The page links forty distinct painting image files. [Stills and photo credits](https://billhamlights.com/light-paintings-screenshots/)

The gallery embeds the following twelve videos in this order. Titles identify eight as silent. Those eight let a performer judge contour persistence, contact, separation and how one exposure fades through another independently of the soundtrack. The online samples document Ham's work across time. A title or upload date does not establish the equipment used in a 1960s performance. The optical rig varied by performance.

| Occasion | Witness and apparatus |
|---|---|
| *Vision in Motion*, January 1966 | Philip Elwood reports watch glasses and dishes over two projection lamps with mirrors and prisms. [Eyewitness account](https://billhamlights.com/reviews-by-philip-e-elwood/) |
| San Francisco studio, January 1967 | Ralph Gleason counted three projectors. Their fades sustained continuity while dishes were rocked and spun. [Studio visit](https://billhamlights.com/reviews-by-ralph-j-gleason/) |
| Museum performance, February 1967 | Alfred Frankenstein estimated six or eight projectors and described a crescendo in pace and brilliance. [Concert review](https://billhamlights.com/reviews-by-alfred-frankenstein/) |

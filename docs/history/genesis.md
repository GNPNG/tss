# Genesis and The Musical Box

Roland Greil, co-designer of Genesis's final tour, identifies oil wheels as part of the band's 1970s lighting history. This retrospective account gives the technique and decade. It leaves the projector, wheel, song, operator and concert open. [Interview with Greil](https://www.ayrton.eu/live/ayrton-karif-shines-through-for-genesis-the-last-domino-tour/)

For *The Lamb Lies Down on Broadway*, Jeffrey Shaw's production archive identifies the original visual artists, Jeffrey Shaw and Theo Botschuijver. Shaw describes three synchronized pairs of Kodak Carousel projectors with one pair per screen across the stage. He reports over two thousand slides projected and illustrates the installation with production images. The oil-wheel attribution above establishes no cue for this particular show. [Designer’s account and images](https://www.jeffreyshawcompendium.com/portfolio/the-lamb-lies-down-on-broadway/)

Montreal's The Musical Box restaged *The Lamb* in 2000. At a June 2001 presentation, Serge Morissette showed how the crew reconstructed the sequence from the surviving, partly disordered slide collection, Robert Ellissehr's concert photographs and amateur 8 mm film. An attendee describes a 90-minute reconstruction synchronized with soundboard recordings, a shorter making-of and a recording of The Musical Box performing its own reconstruction. Denis Gagné also identifies the band's lighting archivist as the source of the slideshow on the 2008 Genesis box set. [Eyewitness report of Morissette's presentation](https://www.genesis-news.com/article/fanclub-event-2001-the-lamb-lies-down-on-broadway-report/) · [Interview with Gagné](https://ultimateclassicrock.com/the-musical-box-genesis-tribute-interview/)

For a Legends recreation, the six outputs require three separately registered screen regions with a dissolve pair in each. Two superimposed program beams can illustrate one pair. Documenting the complete stage layout and song cues requires the additional screen regions and cue programming. Exact lens, lamp, output levels and oil-wheel cues remain unverified in these accounts.
